Usage

Rules.

  • One shape

    Rectangles with --r-md, surface at 60 % + blur, --hairline border. Same height in Background and Projects.

  • Orange is earned

    Section titles, arrows, progress, hovers. Never as a fill, never for body text.

  • Only the arrow acts

    Down = reveal, diagonal = external link, chevron = expand. Cards themselves are not clickable.

  • Two typefaces, fixed roles

    Sonder for anything read as a title or a year; Hanken for everything else. No all-caps except labels.

  • One section per screen

    Each section fills at least the viewport height; the next one never peeks in.

  • Mobile ≤ 680 px

    Centred hero, justified intro, current section name in the closed menu, logo left of the contact info.

  • Accessibility

    Contrast ≥ 4.5:1, visible orange focus, full keyboard support, touch targets ≥ 44 px, alt text.
